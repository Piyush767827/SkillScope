Before you begin

There is no fixed “type” of person

A high-interest area is not automatically a strength. A low score may reflect limited exposure, language, accessibility or unfamiliarity. Use results as clues, not permanent labels.

Keep the comparison fair

When reassessing, use a new task at a similar difficulty, allow comparable time and use the same rubric. A change in score can reflect task difficulty or conditions as well as learning. Keep your work samples so growth can be reviewed.

Privacy and data

This version stores your profile, assessments, plans and portfolio notes in the browser's local storage on this device. It does not send your answers to a server.

Your data stays in this browser unless you export it or share it yourself. Clearing browser data may erase it. Use Export / Backup regularly if you want a copy.

Do not use this prototype for hiring, admissions, diagnosis or other high-stakes decisions. A future multi-user version needs secure authentication, a privacy policy and a proper backend.
